Our Story

More than just
a local cafe.

Nestled on Palace Street, Brothers Ben Cafe was born from a simple idea: to create a space where the Petersham community can gather, connect, and enjoy truly exceptional food and coffee.

We believe in sourcing locally, preparing with absolute care, and serving with a genuine smile. Whether you're grabbing your morning espresso on the go, or settling in for a long weekend brunch, our doors are open.
